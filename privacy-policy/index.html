These Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy explain our facility rules, child registration requirements, our parent-supervised play space, and how we collect, use and protect personal information.

Part 1: Terms & Conditions

1. Our Services

Digging Dreams provides a parent-supervised indoor sandbox play space for children. Parents or legal guardians may register their children, complete the required waiver and consent form, and book a scheduled play session.

Parents and guardians must stay at the facility and remain responsible for supervising their children at all times during the session. Digging Dreams is not a child care service.

2. Parent and Child Registration

Before a child plays, parents or legal guardians must provide accurate registration information, including:

  • Parent or guardian's full name and contact details.
  • Child's full name and age.
  • Emergency contact information.
  • Relevant medical conditions, allergies, or special care instructions.

Parents must ensure all registration information remains accurate and up to date.

3. Parental Consent and Authorization

By registering a child, the parent or legal guardian confirms that they have the legal authority to authorize the child's participation in play activities.

Parents must review and sign the Digging Dreams Waiver, Release of Liability & Consent Form before their child plays.

Parents acknowledge that they will remain at the facility and supervise their child throughout the booked session.

4. Child Supervision and Safety

Digging Dreams is committed to maintaining a safe, clean, and welcoming environment.

Parents and guardians are responsible for supervising their children at all times and must stay in the play space during the session. Our team maintains the facility and follows applicable safety procedures, but our staff do not provide child care or supervise individual children.

Parents must disclose relevant medical conditions, allergies, behavioral concerns, and other information that may affect their child's safety.

Children are expected to follow facility rules and staff instructions, and parents are responsible for helping them do so.

5. Check-In and Leaving

Parents or guardians must complete the check-in process before their children begin play.

A parent or responsible adult must stay with each child at all times. Children may not be left unattended at the facility.

Sessions end at the scheduled time, so please be ready to leave promptly.

If a child is left unattended, staff may ask the parent or guardian to return immediately and may contact the parent or emergency contacts and follow the facility's child-safety procedures.

6. Health and Emergency Situations

Parents must provide accurate information about their child's health, allergies, and medical requirements.

Children who are experiencing contagious illnesses or symptoms that may affect others should not attend.

If a child becomes sick or injured during a session, the parent or guardian is responsible for caring for the child. Staff will help by alerting the parent where needed.

In an emergency, staff may contact emergency services and seek appropriate medical assistance in accordance with applicable law.

7. Reservations and Payments

All play sessions are subject to availability and the booking conditions presented at the time of reservation.

Applicable payment, cancellation, rescheduling, refund, and late pick-up policies will be communicated during booking or before purchase.

Birthday parties, private events, and special bookings may have additional terms and conditions.

8. Facility Rules

Digging Dreams reserves the right to:

  • Enforce facility safety and hygiene rules.
  • Restrict activities that may pose a safety risk.
  • Refuse or discontinue services when behavior creates a safety concern.
  • Temporarily close play areas for cleaning, maintenance, or safety reasons.

Parents and children must respect staff instructions and facility policies.

9. Liability Waiver

Parents or legal guardians must complete the separate Digging Dreams Waiver, Release of Liability & Consent Form before their children play.

Participation in play activities involves certain inherent risks. The separate waiver explains the applicable risks, acknowledgments, and legally permitted liability provisions.

These Terms & Conditions do not replace the separate signed waiver.

Part 2: Privacy Policy

10. Information We Collect

When parents register, make reservations, or use our services, we may collect:

Parent Information

  • Full name.
  • Phone number.
  • Email address.

Child Information

  • Full name.
  • Age or date of birth.

11. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Register parents and children.
  • Manage bookings and play sessions.
  • Check in parents and children.
  • Support the safety of everyone in our play space.
  • Contact parents regarding emergencies.
  • Send booking confirmations and session reminders.
  • Send session-ending notifications.
  • Process payments and provide customer support.
  • Improve our services and website.
  • Meet applicable legal requirements.

12. Email and SMS Text Message Communications

By signing the Digging Dreams Waiver, Release of Liability & Consent Form, parents and legal guardians consent to receive emails and SMS text messages from Digging Dreams about their visit, including booking confirmations, session reminders, session-ending alerts, and important service updates.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

Reply STOP to opt out of text messages or HELP for assistance.

You may opt out of SMS text messages at any time by replying STOP. Opting out will not affect your child's ability to play. To stop receiving emails, please contact us using the details below.

We do not sell or share your mobile phone number or SMS consent information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

13. Sharing of Personal Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share necessary information with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate our business, including:

  • Booking and reservation platforms.
  • Payment processing providers.
  • Website hosting providers.
  • SMS and email communication providers.
  • Customer support and technology providers.

These providers may access information only as necessary to perform services on our behalf.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect the safety, rights, or property of our customers and business.

14. Children's Privacy

Protecting children's personal information is important to us.

Parents or legal guardians provide the information required to register their children and authorize participation in our services.

We do not knowingly encourage children to submit personal information directly through our website.

Where applicable, we follow the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and other relevant children's privacy requirements.

Parents or legal guardians may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their child's personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

15. Data Protection and Retention

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, or disclosure.

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business records, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

16. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, parents or legal guardians may request to:

  • Access their personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information.
  • Update contact information.
  • Withdraw SMS communication consent.

To make a request, please contact us using the details below.

17. Updates to This Policy

We may update these Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any updates will be published on our website with a revised effective date.
