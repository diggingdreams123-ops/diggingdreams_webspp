Clean & Safe

Big imagination. Clean play.

Kids should be free to dig, build and get a little messy, while parents feel confident about the play environment.

🧽

High-Touch Areas

Frequently touched surfaces, tables, seating and door handles are cleaned regularly.

🚜

Toys & Equipment

Play equipment is routinely cleaned. Visibly soiled toys are removed from play until properly cleaned.

🪣

Sandbox Care

Regular maintenance and cleaning, with attention to debris and overall play-area conditions.

🧹

Daily Deep Clean

Our team completes a thorough cleaning routine at the end of each day.

🤲

Hand Hygiene

Handwashing and/or sanitizing stations are available before and after play.

❤️

Sick? Rest at Home

Please stay home if a child or adult has symptoms of a contagious illness.

Cleaner sand. Better play.

About Us

Our story

Digging Dreams was created with one simple idea: children should have a place where they can get messy, get creative and dream BIG.

We believe in play. We believe in imagination. We believe in building memories. We believe parents deserve a place to enjoy it too.
